Privacy

CurbCutter doesn't collect your information. Your reports, photos, and name stay on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send, share, print, or back it up.

Last updated October 11, 2026

What CurbCutter saves, and where

  • Your reports (the place, the problems, your notes, and dates), photos, letters, and the name and contact details you choose to sign with.
  • They're saved on your phone, in CurbCutter's own storage. There is no CurbCutter account, server, or database, so we can't see them.
  • To delete everything, open Settings and tap "Delete all my cases and photos." Clearing your browser's data for curbcutter.org also deletes them. Download a backup first if you might want them later.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

  • Email it opens your email app with the letter filled in. Your email provider handles it from there.
  • Share sends the letter, and photos if you pick that, to the app you choose.
  • Print, Copy, and Download a backup stay with you.
  • Add to my calendar gives you a calendar file for the calendar app you pick. Use Google Calendar instead opens Google Calendar with the reminder's title and date.
  • Links to agencies and organizations, such as the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation or the U.S. Department of Justice, open their websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Location

CurbCutter only uses your location if you tap "Use my location." It saves that spot with your report on your phone and uses it to suggest your area. It isn't sent anywhere. "Check it on a map" opens Google Maps at that spot.

Photos

Photos are shrunk, and their hidden location data is removed before they're saved. They stay inside CurbCutter on your phone and don't go into your photo library unless you save them there.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices built into your phone or browser, and CurbCutter picks an on-device voice when there is one. Some phones use online voices, run by the phone's voice provider.

No tracking

curbcutter.org has no ads, analytics, cookies, or outside scripts. Its built-in security rules stop the app from contacting any other server in the background.

Hosting

CurbCutter's files are delivered by Render, our hosting company. Like any web host, Render may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. Your reports never pass through it.

If you email us

Messages to hello@curbcutter.org reach us through our domain company, Porkbun. We use your message and email address only to reply, and we don't share them.

Children

CurbCutter is for adults and the people who help them. It isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email hello@curbcutter.org.
