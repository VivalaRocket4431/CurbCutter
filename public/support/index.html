Support

Need help with CurbCutter? Start here, or email hello@curbcutter.org.

Get started

  1. On your phone, open curbcutter.org.
  2. Put it on your Home Screen so it opens like an app and works with no signal.
    • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen."
    • Android (Chrome): tap "Install app" when it appears, or open the menu and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen."
  3. Tap "Report a barrier" and answer one question at a time. It saves as you go.

Common questions

Where are my reports saved?

Only on your phone. CurbCutter has no accounts, so we can't recover reports for you. A backup keeps them safe.

How do I move my reports to a new phone?

In Settings, tap "Download a backup." On the new phone, open CurbCutter, go to Settings, and tap "Restore from a backup."

Why aren't my photos in my email?

Email links can't carry photos. On the letter screen, use "Share with photos," or save the photos to your phone and attach them yourself.

Can CurbCutter send the letter for me?

No. You read it, change anything you want, and send it from your own email or phone, so you decide what goes out under your name.

The text is too small, or I'd rather listen.

Settings has three text sizes, high contrast, and dark mode. "Read aloud" at the top of every screen reads it to you.

Does it work without signal?

Yes, after your first visit. Sending an email still needs a connection.

Is this legal advice?

No. For legal help, contact Disability Rights Texas at 800-252-9108. Businesses with ADA questions can call the Southwest ADA Center at 713-797-7171.

I'm outside Texas.

The federal rules in the letters apply everywhere, but the state rules and local contacts are for Texas.

Report a problem

Found a wrong phone number, a broken link, or a confusing step? Email hello@curbcutter.org and say which screen it was on.

Share a success

Did a business fix a barrier after your request? We'd love to hear about it at hello@curbcutter.org. Share only what you're comfortable with.
